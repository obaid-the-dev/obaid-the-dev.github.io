How to choose in 10 minutes

Need onshore staff or regulated data residency?
Pick an onshore firm — Appinventiv, Intellectsoft, Fingent or Musemind.

Building a SaaS MVP or AI agent and want senior engineers fast?
Start with AI Dev Planner. Book the free 30-minute scoping call, get a written scope and fixed quote, and compare it like-for-like.

Need design-led marketing, not product engineering?

Need a local Riyadh / Kuwait team?
Talk to Addicta as well.

Before you pay anyone — anyone, including us — ask these three questions:

  • Will the code live in my GitHub / cloud from day one, with IP assigned?
  • Is the scope written, with exclusions, before payment?
  • What is the warranty after launch?

At AI Dev Planner the answers are yes, yes, and 60–90 days.

Then ask the questions that separate a product team from a brochure team: who exactly will write the code, and can I talk to them directly? How do you handle multi-tenancy, roles and row-level security? For AI features, where is human approval, where are the evaluation sets, and where are the audit logs? What does staging look like every two weeks — a slide, or software I can click?

If an agency cannot answer those clearly, the price does not matter.
